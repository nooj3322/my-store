About Us

Welcome to My Store — a place built around one simple idea: online shopping should feel safe, easy, and honest. We know that trying a new online store can feel risky, which is why we made Cash on Delivery our only payment method. You get to see and check your order before you pay a single rupee.

Our Story

We started with a small selection of everyday products that we genuinely believed in, and a promise to keep things simple: clear pricing, honest product descriptions, and real customer support — not a chatbot, a real person on WhatsApp.

Why Cash on Delivery?

Many of our customers are shopping online for the first time, or simply prefer not to share card details on the internet. Cash on Delivery removes that worry entirely. You order, we deliver, you inspect, you pay. No surprises.

Our Promise

  • Quality products at fair, transparent prices
  • Fast, reliable delivery across the country
  • Friendly support available on WhatsApp and phone
  • Easy returns if something isn't right
