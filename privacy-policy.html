Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how My Store ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us when you visit our website or place an order.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the information you provide at checkout: your full name, mobile number, email address (if provided), delivery address, city, province, postal code (if provided), and any order notes. We do not collect or store any payment card details, because we only accept Cash on Delivery.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order status via phone, WhatsApp, or email
  • To improve our products and customer service
  • To respond to questions you send us via our Contact page or WhatsApp

How We Protect Your Information

Your order details are stored securely and are only accessible to our store administration for the purpose of fulfilling your order. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your shopping cart. This information stays on your own device and is not shared with anyone.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request that we delete your order information, subject to any recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
